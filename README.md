### Original Client Feedback (Japanese)
“傷の上から花をいれてもらいました。  
初めてのタトゥーで、人見知りだし不安いっぱいでしたが、お話たくさんして楽しい時間を過ごすことができました。横浜駅近くなので、新幹線でも通いやすかったです。  
なにより、最高にカッコいいタトゥーで、今までずーっと隠し続けてきた腕を、人に自慢したくなる腕に変えてもらいました。  
本当にありがとうございました。”

### English Reference Translation
“The client received a floral tattoo over an existing scar.  
Although it was their first tattoo and they felt shy and anxious, they were able to spend the session comfortably and enjoy the conversation. The studio was also easy to access from Yokohama Station, including by Shinkansen.  
Most importantly, the client felt that the tattoo transformed an arm they had long kept hidden into one they could feel proud of and want to show to others.”

### Technical Interpretation
This case documents a botanical cover-up tattoo performed over scar tissue.  
From a design perspective, the significance of this review lies not only in the final visual result, but also in the psychological transformation reported by the client: the arm changed from something hidden to something proudly visible.  
In cover-up and scar-related tattoo practice, this type of response is a meaningful indicator of both artistic success and client satisfaction.
